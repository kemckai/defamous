Defamous

Hide celebrity and public-figure posts from your Facebook feed.

Defamous is a free browser extension for Chrome, Edge, and Safari on macOS. It hides posts by public figures, posts and shares about them, and posts Facebook labels as “Public figure”, “Actor”, “Musician”, and similar. Ordinary posts from friends stay put.

Download for Chrome / Edge (.zip) Source on GitHub Privacy policy

Install in Chrome or Edge

  1. Download defamous-extension.zip above and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode in the top-right corner.
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
  5. Open Facebook, click the Defamous icon in the toolbar, and pick your filters.

A Chrome Web Store listing is planned. Safari users can build the macOS wrapper from the safari folder in the repository.

What it hides

  • Posts whose author matches a built-in public-figure name or a name you add.
  • Captions, photo labels, and shares that name one of those people, including a friend passing the post along.
  • Headers whose Facebook category is exactly “Public figure”, “Actor”, “Musician”, “Athlete”, and similar.
  • Optional, off by default: every verified account, high-engagement posts, posts shared from Pages or Groups, and Trending.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Defamous collects nothing, sends nothing, and uses no analytics. Its only permission is storage for your settings, and it runs only on facebook.com.
