Defamous Extension - Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 17, 2025

Introduction

Defamous ("the Extension", "we", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the Extension handles data.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing information.

All filtering operations occur entirely locally within your browser. The Extension does not communicate with any external servers.

Data Storage

The Extension uses Chrome's local storage API solely to save your filter preferences (which filters are enabled/disabled and your engagement threshold settings). This data:

  • Remains on your device
  • Is never transmitted to external servers
  • Is not accessible to us or any third parties
  • Can be deleted at any time through your browser settings

Permissions

The Extension requires the following permissions:

  • Storage: Used to save your filter preferences locally on your device. No data is transmitted externally.
  • Active Tab (implicit): Required to filter content on facebook.com pages. The Extension only runs when you visit Facebook and processes content in real-time without storing it.

No Third-Party Services

The Extension does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not integrate with any external APIs or services that could collect your data.

How It Works

The Extension works entirely through local browser-based filtering:

  • It detects and hides posts on Facebook based on your preferences
  • All processing happens in your browser
  • No information about filtered posts is recorded or transmitted
  • No information about your Facebook activity is collected

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Chrome Web Store developer page or GitHub repository.

Open Source

Defamous is open source, and its code is publicly available for review. You can inspect exactly how the Extension works and verify our privacy claims.
