Defamous

SUMMARY (132 characters max):
Filter out celebrity content from your Facebook feed. Focus on posts from friends and family.

DESCRIPTION (16,000 characters max):
================================================================================

Defamous filters out celebrity and public figure content from your Facebook feed, helping you focus on posts from your real friends, family, and communities.

🎯 WHAT IT DOES

Defamous is a privacy-focused browser extension that removes celebrity noise from Facebook. Instead of seeing verified accounts, public figures, and viral celebrity content, you'll see what matters: posts from your actual friends and family.

✨ KEY FEATURES

• Hide Verified Accounts
  Automatically detects and hides posts from verified accounts (blue checkmarks)

• Filter Public Figures
  Removes content from public figure pages and celebrity profiles

• Block Celebrity Pages & Groups
  Filters out posts shared from celebrity pages, fan pages, and celebrity-focused groups

• Smart Content Filtering
  Detects celebrity-related keywords and filters accordingly (award shows, red carpets, premieres, etc.)

• High Engagement Filter
  Optionally hides viral posts with high engagement that are likely celebrity-related

• Fully Customizable
  Every filter can be toggled on/off through the extension popup. Adjust settings to your preference

• Real-Time Filtering
  Works dynamically as you scroll through your feed

🔒 PRIVACY-FIRST

• No Data Collection
  The Extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data

• Everything Local
  All filtering happens entirely within your browser - no external servers

• No Tracking
  We don't use analytics, advertising, or any third-party tracking services

• Open Source
  The code is publicly available for transparency and review

⚙️ HOW IT WORKS

Defamous uses intelligent detection to identify:
- Verified accounts (blue checkmarks)
- Public figures and celebrities
- Shared posts from celebrity pages/groups
- High-engagement viral content
- Celebrity-related keywords in posts

All filtering is done locally in real-time. Posts are hidden immediately as you browse - no page reload required.

🎛️ CUSTOMIZATION

Click the Defamous icon in your browser toolbar to access settings:
- Toggle filters on/off individually
- Adjust engagement threshold
- Customize which types of content to hide

Perfect for anyone who wants a more personal, less celebrity-focused Facebook experience.

💡 USE CASES

• Want to see more from friends and less from celebrities
• Tired of viral celebrity content dominating your feed
• Prefer authentic connections over influencer marketing
• Want better control over what appears in your feed

📱 COMPATIBILITY

• Chrome (all recent versions)
• Microsoft Edge (Chromium-based)
• Works on facebook.com web interface

⚠️ NOTE

This extension works with Facebook's web interface. It filters content by detecting verified badges, celebrity names, and shared content patterns. As Facebook updates their interface, the extension may need updates.

For best results, refresh your Facebook page after installation and enabling filters.

🔗 TRANSPARENCY

Defamous is open source. You can review the code, verify our privacy claims, and even contribute improvements.
