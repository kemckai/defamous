## Key Points

### ✅ **No Data Collection**
- We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing information
- All filtering operations occur entirely locally within your browser
- The Extension does not communicate with any external servers

### ✅ **Local Storage Only**
- Uses Chrome's local storage API to save filter preferences
- Data remains on your device
- Never transmitted to external servers
- Not accessible to us or any third parties
- Can be deleted anytime through browser settings

### ✅ **No Third-Party Services**
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking services
- No external APIs or services
- No data sharing with third parties

### ✅ **How It Works**
- Detects and hides posts on Facebook based on your preferences
- All processing happens in your browser
- No information about filtered posts is recorded or transmitted
- No information about your Facebook activity is collected

### ✅ **Permissions**
- **Storage**: Used to save filter preferences locally (no external transmission)
- **Active Tab** (implicit): Required to filter content on facebook.com pages
