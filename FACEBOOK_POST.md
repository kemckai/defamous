Tired of your Facebook feed being flooded with celebrity news and verified accounts? I built **Defamous** - a browser extension that filters out all that noise so you can focus on what actually matters: posts from your real friends, family, and communities.

✨ **What it does:**
• Hides verified accounts and public figures
• Filters celebrity content from pages and groups

It's free, open-source, and works entirely in your browser (no data sent anywhere). Perfect for anyone who wants a more personal, less celebrity-focused Facebook experience.

Available for Chrome and Edge. Check it out if you want a cleaner feed! 🔧
